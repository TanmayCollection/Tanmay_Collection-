TANMAY COLLECTION — REAL-WEBSITE STARTER
=============================================

This folder is a functional, mobile-friendly storefront that can be uploaded to a web host.

Included:
- Home page
- Search
- Category filtering
- Product cards
- Shopping cart
- WhatsApp cart checkout
- Contact details
- Responsive mobile layout
- Brand/hero images supplied in the conversation

IMPORTANT BEFORE PUBLIC LAUNCH:
1. Replace the starter catalog entries in script.js with your REAL product names, prices and photos.
2. Remove/replace any demo prices before accepting customer orders.
3. Add your shipping/return/privacy/terms pages.
4. For online card/UPI payments, connect a payment gateway such as Razorpay after your business/KYC setup.
5. For a custom domain, buy your preferred domain and connect it to the hosting provider.

The current site is a static storefront; it does not itself create a domain or hosting account.
